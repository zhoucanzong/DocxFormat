# Chinese official-document formatting

Identify ordinary official-document style, institutional custom redhead, or
formal party/government layout. Record document kind and layout variant
separately: a document named “函” does not alone establish every detail of the
letter-format variant.

Separate issuer area, body, imprint/dispatch area, attachments and page numbers.
Imprint is not a repeated Word footer. Inspect title line breaking, recipients,
hierarchy, attachments, signature/date and last-page placement as distinct roles.

| GB/T 15835-2011 | Numerals in public texts | Current |

Registry status is not full-text clause verification. The 2025 thesis/reference
standard pages do not provide their complete text. Do not fabricate clauses
or silently upgrade the institution's requested version. Verify applicable
clauses against available authoritative text before labeling a rule normative.

For every selected normative rule record its standard/version, clause, conditions,
exceptions, evidence and verification method. Keep institutional requirements,
template observations and user decisions in separate source records. Typical
software line-spacing values are not by themselves national-standard evidence.

If a template conflicts with the intended standard, show the actual difference
and ask whether to reproduce the template or normalize to the standard. Record
approved deviations as custom layout. “All checked applicable rules passed” is
different from “the whole document complies with the entire standard”. Physical
paper/printing/binding requirements cannot be proven from a DOCX alone.

## Interview and execution

- Ask for missing issuer, document number, signer and dates when needed. Never
  use the template's names or today's date as invented facts.
- Resolve redhead fidelity versus standard normalization before component work.
- Apply sparse body/heading rules, explicit section geometry and table choices.
- Keep punctuation/date/number text unchanged unless separately authorized.
- Render first and last pages, odd/even pages, attachment boundaries and any
  landscape pages. Do not estimate final imprint position from paragraph count.
