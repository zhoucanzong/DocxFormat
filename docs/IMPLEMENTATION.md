## Capability boundaries

Preservation, normalization, generation and rebuilding are separate operations.
Existing equations, citation fields, controls, redheads, watermarks and cover
layouts remain intact unless a supported change is explicitly confirmed. This
is not a claim of arbitrary reconstruction or identical pagination in all editors.

Complex structural previews are not native revisions. Approve those components
first, then create a fresh tracked formatting plan on the approved baseline.
Do not reuse an earlier receipt or paragraph locator after structural insertion.

Standards are versioned and source-qualified. Full 2025 thesis/reference-standard
texts were not obtained from their official metadata pages, so detailed clauses
are not invented. The public thesis evaluation proves its stated preservation
and rule scope, not full school/national compliance.

## Publication gate

Rerun the current suite and release checks, inspect the exact staged file list,
exclude local research, downloads, QA artifacts and caches, then verify remote
main after pushing. The active goal is not complete before that verification.
